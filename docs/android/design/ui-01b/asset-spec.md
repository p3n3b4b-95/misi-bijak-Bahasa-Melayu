# Reusable asset specification — UI-01B

Specification for the original illustrations that replace the UI-01 placeholders. It records the art direction taken from Design Board V1, what has been **delivered**, and what is **planned** for later screens so the set stays consistent. Companion files: [`asset-manifest.md`](asset-manifest.md) and [`asset-manifest.csv`](asset-manifest.csv) (dimensions, hashes, provenance, licence status).

## 1. How the artwork was made (provenance)

- **Hand-authored vector art.** Every illustration is an SVG written by hand or generated by a script in this repository (`android/art/svg/`, `android/art/gen/`). No third-party artwork, stock images, icon sets, trace of the board, or AI image generator was used. This session had no image-generation model, only Python, Pillow and headless Chromium.
- **Reference, not copy.** The board was studied for character design, proportions, colours and layout. Nothing was traced or cropped from it (it is a low-resolution flattened picture of unknown provenance).
- **Pipeline:** SVG → headless Chromium (`tools/art/render.cjs`) → lossless WebP in `android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` (`tools/art/build_assets.py`). Re-running the pipeline reproduces the bitmaps and the CSV manifest.
- **Fonts in the logo:** Nunito Black (SIL OFL 1.1), already bundled; the wordmark bitmap embeds its glyph shapes.
- **Authorship statement for the licence decision (Q6):** created by Claude (an AI assistant) for this project in the working session; the repository owner decides the licence. Treat as **project-owned original, licence pending**. Whether AI-authored artwork can carry copyright protection in your jurisdiction is a question for counsel (UNKNOWN).

## 2. Art direction (from the board)

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Look | Cute children's cartoon, bright pastel palette, clean shapes with soft gradients and small white highlights |
| Outlines | Thin (about 0.6% of height) dark warm-brown outline on characters (`#4B2A1B` Amir, `#6B3410` cat, `#5A2B3F` Aina); **no outline on scenery**, which is flat-shaded with light/dark facets |
| Faces | Large round eyes with white highlights, small nose, rosy cheek ovals, simple open or closed smile |
| Heads | Chibi proportion: head about 40–45% of figure height |
| Palette | Board swatches: blue `#0179DC`, pink `#FB688E`, yellow `#FCC328`, green `#86D842`, purple `#AD7FF1`, peach `#FBDCC2`; scenery greens `#4CBB4D…#A8E86A`, sand `#EFC468…#F8DA8C`, mountain lilac/blue `#8EA6EA…#C4CFF6` |
| Backgrounds | Transparent PNG/WebP (alpha), so characters sit on any scene |
| Skin tones | Warm peach family; additional skin tones are a planned extension for the locked characters on the board (screen 3) |
| Accessibility | Characters and scenery are decorative (no `contentDescription`); screens carry the meaning in text. Icon glyphs reach ≥ 3:1 against their gradient (checked by unit test) |

## 6. Technical rules

- **Format:** lossless WebP with alpha, single density in `res/drawable-nodpi` (sizes above are for 3× phone layouts; scenery is scaled up on tablets).
- **Names:** lower-case, underscores, no spaces.
- **Memory:** the Splash screen decodes about 15 MB of bitmaps (largest: `ground` 1800 × 975 and `mountains` 1800 × 570); total asset payload is about 1.1 MB on disk (19 files).
- **Adding a pose:** add an SVG under `android/art/svg/`, add it to `ASSETS` in `tools/art/build_assets.py`, run the build, commit the SVG, WebP and refreshed manifest.
- **Do not** paste board crops into the app.

## 7. Known fidelity gap

The board's art is painterly with subtle texture, rim light and detailed props. These assets are clean vector cartoons that follow the same characters, colours and composition but are **simpler and flatter**. They are original and reusable, not a pixel-accurate reproduction. The UI-01B report lists the visual differences found in real emulator screenshots.
