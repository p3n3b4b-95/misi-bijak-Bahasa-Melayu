## 5. Remaining visual differences (honest)

1. **Finish.** Mine is soft-shaded, textured vector. The board and the references are hand-painted, with painted hair strands, detailed fabric folds, rendered rock faces and painted light scattering. This is the tooling limit stated in art direction §7.
2. **Amir.** He is now full body and standing on the path. The board shows him larger and closer, with his legs hidden by the loading bar, and his face, hair and pose differ in detail. On tablet landscape he is still large (provisional layout).
3. **Cat.** The pose now matches the board (leaning on MULA, paws on the button). Its body is more columnar and its head a little smaller than on the board, and the tail rings are simpler.
4. **Mountains.** They are tiled mirror-wise, so repetition is visible on wide screens. They are snow-capped, whereas the board's are softer and greener. No river or lake (the board's splash has water).
5. **Hut and signpost.** The board's left-hand object is a colourful arch or signpost; mine is a wooden kampung hut and a plain two-arrow signpost (blank boards, so there is no copy to translate). The signpost is hidden behind the cat's tail on the menu.
6. **Title.** The style matches (extrusion, white outer stroke). It sits higher and slightly smaller than on the board. A small cloud touches the bottom of "Bahasa Melayu" on the menu.
7. **Icons.** The gear and person icons match the board's intent. The exit icon is a door-and-arrow, while the board's is a character badge.
8. **Not implemented** (unchanged from UI-01B): the board's menu back arrow and the top status badges.
9. **Aina** is still in the UI-01B flat style and must be redrawn before UI-02 uses her.
10. **Tablet/landscape:** layouts are my own (the board has no such frames) and are marked provisional, as the owner instructed.
